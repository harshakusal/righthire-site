  1. Stage 01JD intake

    Turn the JD into a sharp hiring brief

    We start with your job description and a short call, and turn them into a clear brief: must-haves, nice-to-haves, the real work, deal-breakers, notice and location. When requirements change, the brief is versioned and the search continues from where it is.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Email harshakusalmayuri@gmail.com or call +91 80191 47696.

How fast will I get the shortlist?

Typically within a few days of receiving the JD. Highly specialised roles can take a little longer, and we'll tell you what to expect on the kickoff call.

How do you verify skills?

We start from evidence: code, projects, shipped work and career history. For the must-have skills in your brief, we check claims against that evidence and through a focused technical conversation with the candidate. Anything we couldn't verify is flagged on the candidate's card rather than hidden, so you know exactly what to probe.

How do you avoid duplicate candidates and ownership disputes?

Profiles found on different sources are merged into one person before anyone is contacted, and we won't send you someone you tell us is already in your process. We keep a clear record of when, and for which role, each candidate was introduced, with the candidate's consent, so ownership is never ambiguous.

How do you check availability and notice period?

Before a candidate reaches you, we confirm with them directly: interest in the role, current contact details, location, notice period, earliest joining date and expectations. It's all on the card, so there are no “immediate joiner” surprises after three rounds.

Do you handle interview scheduling and follow-up?

Yes, if you'd like us to. We coordinate slots between the candidate and your panel, confirm in writing, send reminders and handle reschedules. After each round we collect specific feedback and use it to sharpen the next candidates.

Is it all automated?

No, and we won't pretend it is. RightHire uses AI to search and match on evidence, and people to verify skills, talk to candidates and coordinate. We're a young product, and during pilots the founder is hands-on at every stage.

How much does it cost?

The pilot shortlist for one open role is free. Pricing for ongoing work depends on the roles and volume, and we discuss it on a call.

Which roles do you cover?

Tech, engineering and AI/ML roles: backend, frontend, full-stack, mobile, DevOps/SRE, data engineering, ML/AI engineering and engineering leadership, for teams in India and remote/global positions.

How do you handle data and privacy?

We build evidence from publicly available professional information (such as public repositories, portfolios and published work) and from what candidates choose to share with us. Your role details are kept confidential and used only for your search, and candidate details are shared with you only for that role. If you need an NDA, we can discuss one. Read our privacy policy for the details.

Do you work with staffing firms and vendor-management platforms?

Yes. We work as a recruitment partner for staffing firms and vendor platforms that need strong, verified candidates for technical requirements, with clean de-duplication and a clear introduction record. Get in touch and we'll discuss how to fit into your process.
