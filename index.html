Why evidence over résumés

Résumés tell you what people claim. Evidence shows what they've done.

The usual way

Keyword-based screening

  • Matches words on a page, so good writers beat good engineers
  • Misses strong people who don't use the "right" buzzwords
  • Buries you in volume: dozens of résumés to read for each hire
  • Skills stay unverified until late interview rounds

The RightHire way

Evidence-first matching

  • Looks at real work: projects, code, shipped products and career signals
  • Finds capable people wherever they are, including those with thin résumés
  • Hands you only 3–5 strong matches, ranked, with reasons
  • Puts the proof in front of you before the first interview

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Email harshakusalmayuri@gmail.com or call +91 80191 47696.

How fast will I get the shortlist?

Typically within a few days of receiving the JD. Highly specialised roles can take a little longer, and we'll tell you what to expect on the kickoff call.

How much does it cost?

The pilot shortlist for one open role is free. Pricing for ongoing work depends on the roles and volume, and we discuss it on a call.

Which roles do you cover?

Tech, engineering and AI/ML roles: backend, frontend, full-stack, mobile, DevOps/SRE, data engineering, ML/AI engineering and engineering leadership, for teams in India and remote/global positions.

How do you handle data and privacy?

We build evidence from publicly available professional information (such as public repositories, portfolios and published work) and from what candidates choose to share with us. Your role details are kept confidential and used only for your search, and candidate details are shared with you only for that role. If you need an NDA, we can discuss one.

How is this different from a job board or a traditional agency?

Job boards give you volume, and many agencies forward résumés that match keywords. RightHire works the other way round: we look for evidence of real skills first, then hand you only the few candidates who clearly fit, with the reasons attached.

Do you work with staffing firms and vendor-management platforms?

Yes. We work as a recruitment partner for staffing firms and vendor platforms that need strong candidates for technical requirements. Get in touch and we'll discuss how to fit into your process.
